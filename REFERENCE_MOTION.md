# Typeshi Reference Motion

## 1 PRELOADER

Use a full-screen #121613 cover with a centered large 0% counter and a blinking green _ cursor. The counter advances in uneven chunks (0 -> 46 -> 87 -> 100) over about 2.5 seconds. A faint serif Loading... label sits at bottom right. At 100%, the dark cover leaves upward with a curved bottom edge to reveal the hero.

## 2 HEADER

After scrolling past the hero, collapse the wordmark from Typeshi to T with letters sliding out over about 0.5 seconds. Restore the full wordmark at the top. Keep the Menu text and three-bar equalizer at top right.

## 3 CURVED EDGES

Make every boundary between a light and dark section a soft wavy edge using an SVG path rather than a straight cut. At rest the wave is a gentle static shape. While scrolling, let amplitude and phase react to scroll velocity and ease back to rest. Use this at hero->story, featured->approach, approach->dark quote, stats->marquee two, and marquee two->footer. The footer has a bump at the top centre carrying an up-arrow Top of page control.

## 4 MENU

Open with a dark panel sweeping in from the left, using a curved right edge and covering about 65% of the screen. Turn the page behind grayscale with static grain on the remaining right strip. Show Home / Shop / About / Contact as outlined text with a 1px stroke and transparent fill, typed letter by letter with a green _ cursor. The current or hovered link becomes solid white and shows the blinking green _ cursor. Small Email / Instagram links sit below a hairline. A huge, very faint serif Get in touch sits at the bottom. Three short green dashes appear first during opening. Close reverses the sequence.

## 5 PAGE TRANSITION

Use a dark screen with three large green equalizer bars centred and growing or shrinking, then reveal the new page with a curved edge moving up.

## 6 STORY

Use a pinned dark scene. On the left, show a tall portrait image with a vertical Scroll label and a thin line beside it. On the right, show a large statement. Scrolling swaps three scenes: image 1 with statement 1, image 2 with statement 2 (statement scrambles or types in), and image 3 with statement 3 typed with green _ cursors. Keep a small heading, short paragraph, and green button fixed at bottom left.

## 7 MARQUEES

Use large serif and pixel-font words alternating inside the same line, with black-and-white photo tiles between words on a light background. Keep the existing loop, velocity, and skew behavior.

## 8 FEATURED LIST

Use a dark section. Put a large heading, paragraph, and green button on the left. Put three outlined cards on the right with number, product, green arrow, and visit label. As the section scrolls, cards take turns being active. The active card fills near-white with ink content and a slightly wavy top edge. Inactive cards are dim with a thin outline.

## 9 APPROACH

Use a light section with three huge faint circles and faint serif labels. On hover, a circle fills dark with a subtle animated texture only while hovered. On scroll, all three fill dark and overlap; the intersection triangle appears in #2bee4b. Scale the triangle up until it fills the screen green, fade to #121613 while revealing two green chevrons (&laquo; &raquo;) that drift apart, then show a dark scene with a typewriter quote framed by the chevrons.

## 10 STATS

Use a pinned dark section with horizontal scroll. Show outlined rounded boxes with wavy bottom edges, large numerals, and small labels. Each box slides in as the page scrolls sideways.

## 11 TEAM/ABOUT PORTRAITS

Reveal portrait images as a coarse pixel mosaic that resolves to sharp in about six steps. Scramble bio text into place and draw a short green hairline above each name.

## 12 PAGE HEROES (About etc.)

Use a dark hero with a curved bump bottom edge carrying a small Scroll arrow.
